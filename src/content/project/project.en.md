## The challenges

Morocco faces water stress, rising temperatures, energy dependence and the migration of many young people towards major cities. At the same time, families need affordable homes, nearby services and stronger social connections.

## Our solutions

Kasbah3000 combines bioclimatic design, solar generation, water reuse, agroecology and shared mobility. Common spaces, a fabrication workshop, local economic activities and open governance enable every generation to contribute to daily life.

## Project phases

**Phase 1 — Studies and community:** territorial assessment, site selection, consultation and model design. **Phase 2 — Prototype:** construction of the first homes and essential infrastructure, followed by performance monitoring. **Phase 3 — Development:** gradual resident onboarding, expansion of activities and open sharing of lessons learned.

## Our goals

The project seeks to sharply reduce resource needs, improve quality of life and create useful local jobs. It will also produce documented, reusable methods that can inspire other resilient communities throughout Morocco.
