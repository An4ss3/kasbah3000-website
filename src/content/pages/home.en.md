## Our vision

Kasbah3000 envisions a multigenerational ecological settlement rooted in Morocco's landscapes and know-how. We aim to show that low-impact, comfortable and supportive living can address climate challenges while creating lasting opportunities for local communities.

## Our mission

Our mission is to bring residents, researchers, craftspeople, farmers and entrepreneurs together around a pilot site. Renewable energy, responsible water management, local food, bioclimatic architecture and participatory governance are designed as one connected ecosystem.

## Our values

We are guided by respect for life, learning across generations, cooperation and practical experimentation. Every choice should strengthen shared autonomy, inclusion and the project's ability to be adapted elsewhere in Morocco.
