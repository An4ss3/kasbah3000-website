Kasbah3000 welcomes different life paths and combines private homes with inviting common spaces. Children, adults and older residents can meet naturally without giving up their privacy.

Mutual-aid services, meals, celebrations and community projects nurture relationships over time. The community remains open to neighbouring villages and seeks to contribute to the territory rather than become an enclave.
