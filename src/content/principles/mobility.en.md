The centre of Kasbah3000 is designed at pedestrian scale, with shaded, safe and accessible routes. Nearby everyday services reduce the need to use a car for every activity.

Shared bicycles, shuttles and vehicles complement public transport and links to neighbouring communities. Options are adapted to the needs of children, older people, workers and visitors.
