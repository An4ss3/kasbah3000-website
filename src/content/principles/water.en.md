In a context of water stress, Kasbah3000 combines efficient fixtures, leak detection, rainwater collection and low-water landscaping. Usage is monitored to understand demand and reduce losses.

Greywater can be treated through appropriate systems and reused for irrigation in line with health standards. Residents and partners jointly track quality and volumes to continually improve the local water cycle.
