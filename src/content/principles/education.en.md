Workshops, gardens and technical systems provide hands-on learning environments. Children and adults can understand energy, water, food and cooperation by participating in real projects.

Kasbah3000 works with Moroccan schools, universities, craftspeople and associations to host training and research. Useful findings are documented and shared so other places can learn from both successes and mistakes.
