The project favours earth, stone, certified timber and materials from Moroccan supply chains where their performance is suitable. Every choice considers carbon, water, health, durability and end of life.

Prototypes made with craftspeople test assemblies while passing on techniques. Components are designed to be removable, standardised and documented wherever possible, simplifying maintenance and future adaptation.
