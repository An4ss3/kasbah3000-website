Kasbah3000's food gardens use agroecology, compost and varieties suited to Morocco's seasons. They complement produce from neighbouring farms rather than pursuing isolated self-sufficiency.

A shared kitchen and processing spaces support communal meals, preservation and the passing on of recipes. Residents learn to plan crops, reduce waste and make healthy, affordable food part of everyday life.
