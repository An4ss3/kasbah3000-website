Kasbah3000 begins with efficiency: building orientation, climate-appropriate insulation, natural ventilation and efficient equipment reduce consumption without reducing comfort.

Solar installations then produce electricity and hot water close to where they are used. Shared monitoring, carefully sized storage and priority for essential needs strengthen the site's autonomy while retaining a resilient connection to existing networks.
