Kasbah3000 reduces waste at the point of purchase through bulk goods, durable products and shared equipment. A repair workshop extends the life of objects and builds useful skills among residents.

Sorting at source supports composting of organic matter and recovery of other materials with local partners. Simple indicators make progress visible and identify streams that still require new solutions.
