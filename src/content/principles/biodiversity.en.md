Before development, the site's soils, water, plants and wildlife are assessed. Sensitive areas are protected and ecological connections are integrated into the overall plan.

Native species, diverse hedges and minimally sealed ground provide food and shelter for living systems. Participatory monitoring brings residents and scientists together to observe changes and adapt management practices.
