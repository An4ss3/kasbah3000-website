Air quality, thermal comfort, daylight, food and movement are integrated from the design stage. Quiet, active and planted spaces help everyone care for their physical and mental balance.

Prevention is supported by clear information, neighbourly connections and partnerships with local health professionals. Services are designed to remain accessible to children, older people and residents with specific needs.
