Kasbah3000 distinguishes operational, strategic and community decisions so everyone understands how to contribute. Working circles prepare proposals and document trade-offs transparently.

Regular opportunities for information, consultation and decision-making build trust without slowing action. Particular attention is given to less-heard voices, conflict prevention and review of shared rules.
