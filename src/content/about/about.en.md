Kasbah3000 is a Moroccan initiative dedicated to creating a multigenerational ecological settlement. The project brings together expertise in architecture, engineering, agriculture, digital technology, finance and community engagement around one conviction: transition becomes tangible when it improves everyday life.

Our approach begins on the ground. We listen to future residents and local stakeholders, test solutions at a small scale and measure their effects before wider deployment. Moroccan building traditions are combined with research, data and appropriate technologies.

Kasbah3000 aims to become a shared place of learning: somewhere to live, work, teach and welcome others. The project is developed transparently, with respect for its territory and with the ambition to openly share useful lessons.
