## Data we collect

Kasbah3000 only collects information that you choose to provide, for example through a contact form or newsletter registration. This may include your name, email address and the content of your message.

## Use and retention

We use this information to answer enquiries, share project updates and improve our services. It is retained only as long as necessary for those purposes and is not sold to third parties.

## Your rights

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data by using the contact details published on this website. This policy will be expanded before Kasbah3000's public services become operational.
