## What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small file saved by your browser to remember a preference or support a service. Kasbah3000 favours a lightweight website and limits the use of these files.

## Cookies we use

Strictly necessary cookies may retain your language selection, privacy choices or protect a form. Any future audience measurement tools will be configured to minimise collected data and will only be enabled with your consent where required.

## Managing your preferences

You can change or delete cookies through your browser settings. Refusing non-essential cookies will not prevent access to the website's main information.
